# The five rooms: where the codes hide

Each room holds one laptop and five codes. A team of five has ten minutes. That is tight, so most codes are found, not solved: two or three are hidden in a prop and take under a minute, one or two take a short puzzle, and one sits inside the lockbox. The lockbox always holds the code for the room's most important clue, the one that clears a suspect.

Tell the teams one thing before the first rotation: one kid stays on the laptop and reads while the other four search. A team that all searches finds five codes and reads nothing.

## Things that are the same in every room

**Code cards.** Every code is printed on the same small card: a padlock icon, the code in big type, nothing else. Print them on card stock, three copies of each, because kids pocket things. Once a kid has seen two code cards they know what they are hunting for. Codes revealed by a puzzle instead of a card are spelled out below.

**Emily's mark.** A small hand-drawn camera, the size of a thumbnail. It marks the one item that matters among look-alikes. The kids learn it in the party room on the domino, and it comes back twice here. Never use the silver star for anything Emily made.

**One piece of equipment per room.** The steamer lives in the bedroom. The UV light lives in the classroom. Rooms run at the same time, so nothing can be shared.

**Hints.** Hold them for the first four minutes. After that, one hint per code, using the lines below. At eight minutes, point.

### The photo wall

Eight printed photos of Emily's life: family, church, youth group, a selfie with Zulmarie, a shot from behind her camera. Each one has a date handwritten in the bottom corner the way people date prints, and one capital letter in the opposite corner, small, like a sorting mark. Hang them on the wall out of order with removable clips or hooks so they can be moved.

Sorted by date, oldest to newest, the letters read **P R A C T I C E**.

Spread the dates across years so the sorting is obvious once they notice: 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024, Jun 2026, Sep 2026. Make two of the photos look very similar so a kid who tries to sort by content gets stuck and looks at the dates instead.

Hint: "The wall is out of order." Second hint: "Look at the corners."

### The mirror and the Bible, and the lockbox

Before the first team, write **PSALMS** on the vanity mirror with a fingertip dipped in a little dish soap, let it dry invisible, and wipe the mirror lightly. Steam from the steamer brings it up. Leave the steamer on the vanity, switched off, like a hair tool.

In Emily's Bible, one verse in the Psalms is underlined in pencil with a tiny camera drawn in the margin: **Psalm 27:1**. Nothing else is marked. Psalms is long enough that flipping for it takes a minute.

Lockbox combination: **271**, chapter and verse. Inside: the code card for `2306`.

Set the lockbox on the vanity next to the Bible so the three things sit together.

Hint: "Her mirror fogs up every morning." Second hint: "Chapter, verse."

### The microphone

A handheld mic with a grille that unscrews. Roll the `0917` card tight and drop it in the head. Screw the grille back on. Leave the mic on the bed like she dropped it there.

Hint: "She sings. Check the mic."

### The drawer and the pillow

`6642` card taped flat to the underside of the drawer, so the drawer has to come all the way out or someone has to reach under. Put normal things in the drawer: a hairbrush, a charger, a lens cloth.

`4471` card inside the pillowcase, pushed to the far end. The pillow sits on a made bed.

Hints: "Take the drawer out." / "Nobody makes their bed that neatly."

### Reset

- Re-shuffle the photos on the wall. Different wrong order each time.
- Re-steam the mirror only if the soap word has been wiped off. Otherwise wipe the condensation and it will reappear next steam. Keep a soap dish behind the mirror.
- Lock the box, scramble the dials, put the `2306` card back inside.
- Screw the card back into the mic head. Re-tape the drawer card. Re-stuff the pillow. Make the bed.

### The stopped clock and the lockbox

A battery wall clock with the battery out and the hands set to **7:59**. Hang it where a clock would be. Next to the lockbox, a slip in Emily's handwriting: "the clock stopped when the lights went out".

Lockbox combination: **759**. Inside: the `5190` card.

Hint: "What time is it in this room?"

### The mirrored note

Print `LIVESTREAM` mirrored, so it reads **MAERTSEVIL** on paper, in a plain font, and fold it into the drawer with a few bulletins and pens. Holding it up to the mirror reads it the right way round. Kids who read it backwards without the mirror still get it, which is fine.

Hint: "Some things only make sense in a mirror."

### The guitar

Roll the `7788` card and tape it inside the body through the sound hole, where a phone flashlight shows it but a glance doesn't. Leave the guitar on its stand.

Hint: "Look inside the guitar."

### The offering plate and the picture

A stack of offering envelopes in the plate, all blank except one with "Emily" written on the front. Inside, the `6271` card.

The `1130` card taped flat to the back of one framed picture on the wall. Two or three pictures, so they have to check more than one.

Hints: "Somebody left an offering." / "Pictures have backs."

### Reset

- Lock the box, scramble, card back inside. Check the clock still says 7:59.
- Fold the mirrored note back into the drawer under the bulletins.
- Card back in the guitar, card back in the envelope, envelope back in the stack, card back behind the picture.

### The verse cards

Seven printed verse cards scattered around the room: on the music stand, taped to the wall, on the drum throne, leaning on the amp. Each has a verse and its reference. On six of them, one word is highlighted and the back has a number 1 to 6. Sorted by number, the first letters of the highlighted words spell **H O O D I E**.

Pick verses with usable words, for example: **H**ope (Romans 15:13), **O**pen (Revelation 3:20), **O**ffer (Romans 12:1), **D**oor (John 10:9), **I**n (Psalm 46:1, "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help **in** trouble"), **E**very (Philippians 4:6).

The seventh card has no highlight and no number. It has a small camera drawn in the corner. Its reference is **Psalm 46:1**, same verse as card five, printed again on its own. That's the lockbox card.

Lockbox combination: **461**. Inside: the `5502` card.

Hints: "The verses have numbers on the back." / "First letters." / For the lockbox: "One card has her camera on it. Chapter and verse."

### The bass, the mic, the drawer

`3141` card in the gig bag's front pocket, under a cable.

`9312` card folded small under the foam windscreen of one mic. Three mics on stands, one with foam.

`CONES` card under the paper liner at the bottom of the drawer. Put setlists and picks on top of the liner.

Hints: "Winston plays bass." / "One mic is dressed differently." / "The drawer has a bottom under the bottom."

### Reset

- Lock the box, scramble, card back inside.
- Re-scatter the seven verse cards in new spots.
- Cards back in the bag pocket, under the foam, under the liner.

### The schedule

Emily's weekly schedule, printed as six separate strips, one per period, pinned to the wall out of order. Each strip has a time, a subject and a room number. Five strips have a room number. The lunch strip doesn't.

Sorted by time, the room numbers read **8, 8, 2, 3**, and lunch sits in the middle with no number.

```
7:45   Homeroom        Rm 8
8:30   English 9       Rm 8
10:15  Lunch
11:00  English 10      Rm 2
12:30  Yearbook        Rm 3
2:00   Planning        (no room, "office")
```

Make the planning strip say "office" so there are exactly four digits and the kids aren't tempted to type five.

Hints: "Her day is out of order." / "Read the room numbers in order."

### The whiteboard and the lockbox

Written on the board in Emily's hand under the heading "warm up":

```
(12 × 30) + 47 = ?
```

That is **407**. The lockbox sits on the teacher's desk. Inside: the `7034` card.

Leave markers and the eraser in the tray. If a kid erases the board, the leader rewrites it from this sheet.

Hint: "Do the warm up."

### The exam papers and the UV light

A stack of eight graded exams on the student desk. On one of them, in the margin of the last page, write `6060` in invisible ink. In the drawer of the teacher's desk, the UV flashlight and a sticky note that says "grade these", with nothing else that needs UV in the room.

Hints: "There's a flashlight in the drawer. It isn't a normal flashlight." / "She didn't finish grading."

### Under the desks

`4807` taped under the student desk. `1899` under the desk mat or keyboard on the teacher's desk.

Hints: "Students hide things under desks." / "Teachers hide things on top of desks, under something."

### Reset

- Re-shuffle the schedule strips.
- Lock the box, scramble, card back inside. Check the board still has the warm up.
- Exam with the UV note back into the middle of the stack. UV light back in the drawer.
- Cards back under both desks.

### The menu, the receipt, and the lockbox

Print a one-page IHOP-style menu with about a dozen items and prices. On the table, a receipt with no prices on it, just the items:

```
Table 7
1  Chocolate chip pancakes, no whipped cream
1  Hash browns
1  Hot tea
```

Chocolate chip, no whipped cream is Emily's order. Prices on the menu: pancakes **$4.50**, hash browns **$3.25**, hot tea **$1.75**. Total **$9.50**.

Lockbox combination: **950**. Inside: the `9163` card.

Keep the menu prices to quarters so the sum is clean, and don't let any other three items on the menu add up to $9.50.

Hints: "She always orders the same thing." / "Add it up."

### The ticket rail

In the kitchen, five handwritten order tickets on a rail or clipped to a string. Each has a table number, items, and a ticket number at the top. Only one matches the receipt on the table, item for item, and its ticket number is **2290**. The other four are close but wrong: whipped cream on the pancakes, a different table, coffee instead of tea.

Hint: "Which ticket is hers?"

### The silverware roll-ups

Seven napkins rolled around plastic forks and knives, in a bus tub by the kitchen. Inside each, a slip with a number and a letter: 1 L, 2 A, 3 N, 4 T, 5 E, 6 R, 7 N. Unroll, sort, read **LANTERN**.

Hint: "Nobody rolls silverware that carefully."

### Under the table and in the syrup

`4455` taped under the table. `3579` rolled tight inside a clean empty syrup bottle, so it rattles. A fork gets it out.

Hints: "Under the table." / "What's in the syrup?"

### Reset

- Lock the box, scramble, card back inside. Receipt back on the table, menu back in its stand.
- Tickets back on the rail in a new order.
- Re-roll the seven napkins.
- Card back under the table, card back in the bottle.
