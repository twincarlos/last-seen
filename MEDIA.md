# Last Seen: story and unlockables

Friday October 30. The carnival reveal. Zulmarie gathers everyone for a photo, Carlos counts down, and on three the room goes black. When the lights return, Emily is gone. Minutes later her number posts "I'm sorry. I took it. Tell Pastor Will I'm sorry. Don't look for me." Annee opens the carnival account and says it is empty. The 4707 Carnival + Movie Night Fair is tomorrow.

The truth: Annee runs the carnival and brought in Smash n' Go. Julen, who runs the truck, is her partner. They have done this before, at another church, in 2019. Annee had Emily put on the carnival account so the money trail would end at her. Emily found the 2019 photo, pulled the edit history, and was going to tell Will after service. Annee had Melo build a timed blackout "for the reveal." Julen opened the side door with the load-in code and walked Emily out. She is in the back room of the Smash n' Go commissary on Dock Street.

Each team visits all five rooms, ten minutes each, in a different order. No room names Annee on its own. Each room gives one fact that fits two or three people. Only all five together fit one.

## How it solves

- The payee was changed Monday at 8:31 PM under the shared committee login (Gabriel's room). The committee login is Annee and Zulmarie. On the practice recording (Melo's room) Zulmarie never stops leading, and the keys drop out at 8:28. The kids know who plays keys.
- The side door opened from outside at 7:59 with the load-in code (Winston's room). Annee gave that code to Julen (Setup chat). The truck was in the lot from 7:45 to 8:03 (Winston's room).
- Julen told Will he started last year. Emily's 2019 photo shows him at Harvest Fest under another truck name with someone cropped out (Zulmarie's room). The uncropped version on Annee's laptop shows who.
- Emily's lanyard and camera are on the ground outside the side door (Winston's room). She did not run. Her 6:50 PM memo says so in her own voice (Zulmarie's room).
- Where: the commissary address is at the bottom of the real invoice (Gabriel's room). The back room with a cot is in Julen's video (Annee's room).

Answer: Annee and Julen. The Smash n' Go commissary. Emily never took a cent.
